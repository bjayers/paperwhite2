# Migration map

Drafted 2026-11-13 for the birds' batch (`birds.yaml`); other batches follow it where Pokémon move with the seasons
(bugs, fish, ice Pokémon). It's the story first: each bird's homes in its entry carry the seasons drawn here.

## The flyways

- **The coastal flyway** (east and south): Route 18 (the island stopover) → Route 1 → Routes 2 and 3 → the Southwest
  valley (Routes 19 and 20) inland, or up the east coast (Route 13, Route 22) to the north.
- **The valley flyway** (west): the Southwest's valley (Route 19, Floccesy, Route 20) → Route 6's river (upstream of
  Route 20, as the user likes to think) → Route 7 → Icirrus.
- **The central passage**: Route 5 and Route 16 → Route 15 and Route 14 → Route 12 and Village Bridge → Route 11.
  Marvelous Bridge and Driftveil Drawbridge are its two crossings, where birds pass in the shadows.

## Predators and prey along the way

- **Swellow follow Yanma** (Pokécology): where Yanma hatch over the marshes in summer (Route 7, Pinwheel's outskirts,
  Route 3, Route 6's puddles), Swellow arrive; the bugs' batch makes Yanma rarer where Swellow are common.
- **Pidgeotto and Staraptor** hunt the small birds and rodents of the fields; **Braviary and Mandibuzz** the desert's
  (Sandile, Trapinch, Cubone; the desert batch).
- **Hoothoot and Noctowl** hunt the barns' and fields' rodents at night (Patrat, Rattata), when the day's hawks sleep.
- **Murkrow** follow people, and Honchkrow lead their flocks at night.
