# The family dictionary

One entry per evolutionary family: what it is, where it lives and why. The entries are drafts for the user's review;
the generator (still to write) turns them, with `exceptions.yaml` and the places, into
`res/paperwhite2/encounters/species/*.json`, checking and balancing but never inventing placements.

- `skeleton.yaml`: generated by `tools/families.py` from `res/` (members, evolutions, types, base stats, Pokédex text,
  the game's gifts, static battles, trades and Hidden Grottoes, and the hack's current tables). Don't edit it.
- `batches.yaml`: which batch file holds each family; every family is in exactly one list.
- `migration.md`: the birds' region-wide map of who is where in each season, which other batches follow too.
- `<batch>.yaml`: the entries, with a summary at the top.
- `report.md`: the balance pass, the review list and the special-encounter suggestions.

## An entry

```yaml
- id: pidove                     # skeleton.yaml's id (its first stage)
  origin: native                 # native | migrant | introduced | ancient (rules.md, 3)
  lore:                          # what the placement rests on, most important first (rules.md, 2)
    - "B2W2 Pokédex (Pidove): ..."
    - "Real world: feral pigeons ..."
  habitats: {city: 3, suburb: 3, park: 3, farmland: 2}   # vocabulary.yaml's biomes, 3 = its best, 1 = it gets by
  time: {periods: day, strength: preferred}   # day | night | any (or a list of periods); strict | preferred | none
  seasons: all year              # a sentence: when, and how it changes
  migration: resident            # resident | partial | summer | winter | passage | nomad
  weather: {more: [], less: [rain], only: []}   # rain, gusty_rain, thunderstorm, snow, light_snow, blizzard,
                                 # diamond_dust, sandstorm: a tier up or down (rules.md, 7); only = weather-only
  abundance: common              # its tier in its best homes
  trophy: []                     # places where it's the exotic (trophy) Pokémon
  battles: {single: 85, double: 12, triple: 3, partners: family}   # partners: family | area
  stages:                        # each member: wild or not, and why when not obvious
    pidove: wild
    tranquill: wild
    unfezant: {wild: true, note: "the flock's leaders, rarer"}
  levels: {}                     # overrides only; the default rule is below
  wanderer: {appears: both, wanders: random, grass: stand}   # appears both|steps|overworld; wanders
                                 # random|aggressive|passive; grass stand|float
  homes:                         # the placements, each with its reason
    - {place: route_19, methods: [grass], seasons: all, times: day, tier: common, why: "..."}
  review: ["..."]                # the calls the user should check
```

- **Homes** name a place of `places/*.yaml` (the generator expands it to its areas) and, where a place's parts differ,
  the method that reaches the part (Route 1's dark grass is its pine hills). `seasons` is `all` or a list of
  spring, summer, autumn, winter; `times` is `any`, `day` (morning, day, evening), `night` (night, late night) or a
  list; left out, it's the family's `time` (the generator applies its strength: strict keeps it to its periods,
  preferred makes it a tier rarer outside them). `tier` is the family's tier there as a whole: the generator gives a
  later stage one tier lower than its base unless the entry says otherwise, and splits by level through the level
  gate. A `stages:` list in a home limits it to those members (Swanna only, in winter). `weather` in a home makes it weather-only there; `form` sets the form (Basculin 0 Red-Striped, 1
  Blue-Striped; Shellos 0 West Sea, 1 East Sea).
- **Outbreaks** (optional): `outbreaks: [{places, seasons, stages, why}]`, a day when the family takes the outbreak
  share of those places (rules.md, 10, still tentative).
- `areas: [...]` in a home limits it to some of the place's maps (Virbank Complex 2 only).
- `homes_by: settings` marks a wild family placed by the settings rather than homes (Audino's share of shaking
  grass).
- **Rippling water**: a rippling table with nothing of its own in a season and time takes the same water's plain
  table (surf or fishing) then, favouring the later stages (a generator rule drafted 2026-11-14, for the user's
  approval). Rippling homes of a family's own come first.
- **Stages** that aren't wild stay out of every home. **Partners** `area` means any Pokémon of that area may join a
  double or triple.
- **Levels,** by default (rules.md, 5): a first stage from 2 to its evolution level + 3; a later stage from the level
  it evolves at to 100, and to its own evolution level + 3 when it evolves again; for a stone, friendship or other
  evolution, a guess at the level a wild one would have it (stated in `levels`). Babies cap lower.
- **Tiers:** common, uncommon, rare, very_rare, exotic (`system.md`). Exotic is the trophy tier (rules.md, 8).
- Check the files with `python3 tools/dictionary_check.py` (from this folder); `--counts` and `--report` print, per
  place and method, how many families and which live there in each season. `python3 tools/balance.py` is the balance
  pass (empty, crowded and sparse tables by season and day or night, type mix, the Nature Preserve rule).
- `review` lists every uncertain call; `report.md` gathers them for the morning.
