# Weather plan

For the Weather Editor (editor/README.md, The Weather Editor): each place's weather now, the user's wishes, and the
suggestions made for placements, region by region. Percentages are the share of a season's days. Indoor zones (caves,
sewers, towers, Relic Castle, the Dreamyard's basement) can't have weather. A weather in battle also changes the
field's for 5 minutes. Weathers: rain, gusty rain, thunderstorm, snow (flake; flutter is the light snow), blizzard,
diamond dust, sandstorm.

anyway. Routes 14 and 15 are treated as usual.
