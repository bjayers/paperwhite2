assessment of each: the vision, what I'm satisfied with, what I'm not or am unsure of, and ideas. The tables are
generated by `python3 tools/place_reviews.py` (run it again after any change); the assessments are in
`assessments.yaml`.

## What comes up again and again

- **Places with nothing common** (Route 11, Route 22, Victory Road's mountainside, the Desert Resort's path, P2
  Laboratory, the Dreamyard): every Pokémon is equally likely, so none feels like the place's own. One or two
  signature commons each would help.
- **Grass and dark grass nearly the same** (Route 20, Lostlorn, Victory Road's forest, Dragonspiral's grounds): the
  dark grass could keep the shyer or stronger ones.
- **Thin fishing** (Aspertia, Route 4, Virbank City, Route 17, Undella outside summer): often one family.
- **Thin tables** where the place deserves more: Route 14's grass, Route 13's inland grass, the Damp Caves, the
  Strange House by day, Dragonspiral Tower, Victory Road's ruins, Castelia's sewer walkways.
- **Several trophies in one place** (Chargestone Cave, Victory Road's caves, Castelia's park, Abundant Shrine, Route 7,
  Virbank Complex): each trophy means less when it shares the place.
- **Crowded grass** (Floccesy Ranch, Routes 5, 19, 23, the Desert Resort, Giant Chasm's crater, Reversal Mountain).
- **The Nature Preserve** isn't yet the reward it should be: it's the place I'm least satisfied with.
- **Your own descriptions I drifted from**: Route 19's Swinub and Shroomish (you said rare), Floccesy's NPC seasons,
  and Castelia's park list (Snubbull, Meowth, Minccino).
